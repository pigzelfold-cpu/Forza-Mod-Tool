Save Swap - Forza Horizon 5 & Forza Horizon 6

Close the game and back up your entire save folder first. Originals are never overwritten.

1. DONOR SAVE: the FH5 ProfileData (latest, v1.619.349.0, or legacy 2021-2023 build) or FH6 C_ProfileData with the progress you want.
2. TARGET SAVE: your own FH5 ProfileData or FH6 C_ProfileData (click Find Save... to locate installed saves automatically). Double-check the order.
3. Build Swapped Save writes a verified .swapped file encrypted in your target save's exact format and account identity (outside live SystemAppData\wgs folders for MS Store saves).
4. Replace your target save file with a renamed copy of the .swapped output while the game is closed. Keep your backup.

The donor supplies progress; the target supplies account identity (XUID) and encryption format/IV. Older downloaded FH5 donor saves are automatically re-encrypted for your target FH5 version.

Save locations:
- FH5 Steam: <Steam>\userdata\<steamId>\1551360\remote\<xuid>\User_<hex>.ProfileData
- FH5 MS Store / Xbox App: %LOCALAPPDATA%\Packages\Microsoft.624F8B84B80_8wekyb3d8bbwe\SystemAppData\wgs\<account>\<container>\<guid>
- FH5 OnlineFix / EMPRESS: C:\Users\Public\Documents\OnlineFix\1551360\Saves\... or C:\Users\Public\Documents\EMPRESS\1551360\...
- FH6 Xbox / Retail (PGS): C:\XboxGames\GameSave\pgs\u_<account>_<suffix>\current\ContainersRoot\User_<account>\C_ProfileData

Both donor and target saves must be from the same game (FH5 -> FH5, or FH6 -> FH6).
